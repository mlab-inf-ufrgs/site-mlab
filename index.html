About MLAB

MLAB comprises researchers and students who share a common interest in AI and its potential to transform fields such as healthcare.

We develop machine learning methods to address real-world challenges in healthcare. At the same time, we investigate the limits and foundations of these methods, advancing the conceptual and methodological groundwork to create more rigorous and robust solutions.
